## ⚔️ WHAT IS THIS?

**D&D Monster Battle Simulator** is a fully-featured combat arena where legendary creatures from the Dungeons & Dragons universe clash in epic battles. Built with authentic **D&D 5th Edition** mechanics, this simulator brings the thrill of tabletop combat to your browser.

Watch an **Ancient Red Dragon** unleash its devastating fire breath upon a **Lich**, or pit a team of **Mind Flayers** against the terrifying **Tarrasque**. Every attack roll, saving throw, and damage calculation follows official D&D 5e rules.

## 🎲 D&D 5e COMBAT MECHANICS

```
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │                         COMBAT ROUND FLOW                              │
  │                                                                        │
  │   ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐    ┌──────────┐        │
  │   │INITIATIVE│───►│  ATTACK  │───►│  DAMAGE  │───►│  EFFECTS │        │
  │   │  d20+DEX │    │ d20+MOD  │    │ XdY+MOD  │    │   SAVE   │        │
  │   └──────────┘    └──────────┘    └──────────┘    └──────────┘        │
  │                                                                        │
  │   Roll d20        Attack vs AC    Roll damage     Apply conditions     │
  │   + DEX mod       ≥ AC = HIT      on successful   Target may save      │
  │   High goes       NAT 20 = CRIT   hit. Crits      vs spell effects     │
  │   first           NAT 1 = MISS    double dice                          │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Fully Implemented:**
- ✅ Initiative rolls (d20 + DEX modifier)
- ✅ Attack rolls vs Armor Class
- ✅ Critical hits (natural 20) and fumbles (natural 1)
- ✅ Damage types (fire, cold, lightning, necrotic, radiant, etc.)
- ✅ Damage resistances, immunities, and vulnerabilities
- ✅ Saving throws (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA)
- ✅ Multiattack actions
- ✅ Legendary actions (3 per round for legendary creatures)
- ✅ Legendary resistances (auto-succeed saving throws)
- ✅ Recharge abilities (breath weapons, etc.)
- ✅ Condition effects (frightened, paralyzed, stunned, etc.)
